Why this works

The critical positional reference points (VDO, tooth location, hinge/occlusal relationship) are preserved from the diagnostic wax-up—reducing guesswork and chairside variability.

THE ZYLO DIFFERENCE

A provisional is only valuable if it’s repeatable. Wax-ups and conversion prostheses don’t help acceptance or outcomes when they’re one-off “doctor art projects.” Zylo compresses the chain into a delegatable, standard workflow.

Typical setup

Manual variability + training burden

  • Denture conversion on articulator (slow, error-prone)
  • Intraoral cylinder pick-up (position + tissue pressure guesswork)
  • Higher fracture risk and remake loops
  • Hard to standardize across assistants/shifts
Zylo setup

Preserved references + automated production

  • ZyloCAD — AI-assisted design (wax-up references preserved)
  • ZyloPrep — one-click file prep
  • ZyloDent — print + wash + cure (automated)
  • Consistent outcomes across assistants
