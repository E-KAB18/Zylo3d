The reality

If you already scan digitally, why are splints still a multi-day process?

If you already own an intraoral scanner, manufacturing shouldn’t be the bottleneck. Yet outsourcing splints to a third‑party lab often means ~5 days of turnaround and about $100 per unit—plus remakes that steal chair time. Many guards are still thermoformed without a true 3D bite scheme, so fit and comfort can vary.

Bringing splints in‑house improves margins and patient satisfaction, but most stacks require CAD, file prep, a printer, a washer, and a curing unit—and a skilled operator to keep it all running. Zylo integrates the missing steps: ZyloCAD and ZyloPrep streamline design and file prep with AI, and ZyloDent automates print–wash–cure in one cabinet‑ready footprint so your assistant can produce consistent splints with minimal manual steps.

How it works

From scan to splint in ~60 minutes

A delegation-first workflow: AI design, one-click prep, then automated print–wash–cure.

1) ZyloCAD (AI-assisted)
Upload upper/lower + bite → generate a splint/guard; adjust thickness, relief, and contact strategy.
~10 min
2) ZyloPrep
Auto-orient + place supports away from functional surfaces → apply validated profiles by material.
~3 min
3) ZyloDent (All-in-one)
Print → automated transfer → wash → cure in one device.
~40–45 min
4) Finish / Polish
Remove supports, quick polish, and final fit check.
~5–10 min
Total: ~60 minutes
Varies by arch coverage + material.
